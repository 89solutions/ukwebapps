Lunch Rush

It’s noon and the queue is building. Grill the burgers, fry the chips and keep everyone fed at The Toffee Apple food truck. How long can you last?

Works on phones, tablets and computers. On a keyboard: G grill a burger, F fryer, Space pick up what’s cooked, letters for the shelf, 1–4 serve, Esc pause.

How to play

  1. Read the order. Each customer shows what they want in a speech bubble. Their bar shows how patient they are.
  2. Cook. Tap Burger or Sausage to put one on the grill, and tap a fryer basket for chips. Pick them up when they say Ready. Leave them too long and they burn.
  3. Build the tray. Add buns, rolls, cheese, onions, pop and toffee apples from the shelf. Tap anything on the tray to bin it.
  4. Serve. When the tray matches an order, that customer’s bubble glows green. Tap them to serve. Quick service earns bigger tips.
  5. Survive the rush. Each day runs from 12 till 2. New food arrives as the days go on, and the queue gets busier. Three walk-outs and the hatch closes.

Your best score is saved on your device, so you can keep trying to beat it.

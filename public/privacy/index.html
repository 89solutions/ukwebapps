Privacy policy

Last updated 28 November 2026

Toffee Apple Web (trading as Toffee Apple Web) is responsible for personal information collected through this website. Questions? Email info@uk-web-apps.co.uk.

What we collect and why

  • Contact form: your name, phone, email and message, so we can reply to you. Messages are sent to us by text message and email.
  • Purchases: Paddle collects your payment details and billing information as Merchant of Record; we never see your card details. Paddle shares your email address and order number with us so we can send your download link and help with any problems.

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to deal with them, and order records for up to 6 years for accounting purposes.

Who we share it with

Paddle (payments), our web host, and our email and SMS providers, only as needed to provide our service. We never sell your information. See Paddle’s privacy policy for how Paddle handles your data.

Cookies

This website doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies. Paddle’s checkout may set cookies needed to process your payment securely. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Our apps

Our apps such as Van Stock store your data on your own device. It isn’t sent to us.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email us and we’ll respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

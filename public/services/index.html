SMS & voice systems

Text message and phone systems: bookings, alerts, receipts and masked calls.

We’ve been building with text messages and phone calls for years. They reach everyone, including customers who never install an app.

See it in action: Tea and Company

  • SMS bookings and enquiries
  • Two-factor login codes by text or call
  • Website monitoring alerts
  • Number-masked calls and texts
  • SMS bookingsCustomers text a date and time; the system checks availability and replies straight away.
  • SMS queriesCustomers text a keyword, the database is searched and the answer comes back by text.
  • SMS receiptsSend a lasting receipt by text instead of paper, and keep the customer’s number.
  • Send2CustomerWhile on the phone, press a button to text key details straight to the caller. No more pen hunting.
  • 2-factor authenticationSecure logins with a one-time code sent by SMS or voice call.
  • Website monitoringWe watch your site around the clock and text or call you if anything goes wrong.

  • Branded to match your shop
  • Fewer “is mine ready?” questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what you need, from a simple one-page site for a tradesperson to a full web app. Tell us about your project and we’ll send a clear, no-obligation quote.

How long does it take?

A small business website usually takes a few weeks from first chat to going live. Web and mobile apps take longer, and we agree a plan with you at the start.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every site we build is set up for search: fast pages, proper titles and descriptions, structured data that tells Google what your business does and where, a sitemap, and Google Search Console.

Do I own my website?

Yes. You own the content and the code. We build with plain, standard technology so you’re never locked in to us or to a page-builder subscription.

Can you update or rescue my existing site?

Yes. We’ve moved businesses from Wix and older sites onto faster, better-looking ones, and we can keep your existing domain and email.

Do you only work with businesses in Newcastle?

We’re based in the North East and love working with local businesses, but we work with clients anywhere in the UK by phone and video call.

Got an idea, or a website that isn’t pulling its weight?

Tell us what you need. We’ll give you honest advice and a clear, no-obligation quote.

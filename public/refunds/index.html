Refund policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

We want you to be happy with your purchase. If you’re not, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of buying, for any reason.

How to ask for a refund

Email info@uk-web-apps.co.uk with your order number (it starts txn_ and is on your receipt), or reply to your Paddle receipt email. You can also request a refund directly from Paddle at paddle.net.

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle provides all customer service inquiries and handles returns.

Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 working days. After a refund, please delete your copy of the app.

After 14 days, you still have your full rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 if the app is faulty or not as described.

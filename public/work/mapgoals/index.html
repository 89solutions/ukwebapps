The project

About MapGoals

Losing 30lbs feels abstract. Walking from Newcastle to Las Vegas doesn’t. MapGoals lets people pick a start and a destination, set a goal, and watch a little runner, cyclist, car or plane move along the route as they log progress.

Behind the fun is careful maths: exact unit conversions, live currency rates, and a forecast finish date that updates every time progress is logged.

How it’s built

Under the bonnet

  • JavaScript
  • Leaflet maps
  • OSRM routing
  • ECB currency rates
  • Local storage
  • Automated tests
Key features

What MapGoals does

Your goal as a route

Choose “as the crow flies” or a real driving route. The traveller moves along it in proportion to your progress.

Estimated finish date

Based on your average rate so far, with a finish-by date showing whether you’re ahead or behind.

Any unit you like

Weight, money, distance, time or your own unit. Log in stones towards a lbs goal, or euros towards a £ goal.

Private by design

Everything stays in your browser. No account, no server and no tracking.

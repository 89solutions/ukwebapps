The project

About Whitfield Electrical Services

Whitfield Electrical Services covers everything from full house rewires, fuse boxes and EICRs to EV chargers, home cinema, CCTV, data cabling and KNX smart homes. They needed a site that ranks locally, explains that range clearly and turns visitors into phone calls.

We built a quick-loading single-page site with clear calls to action, a gallery of their work, the areas they cover and a FAQ, all marked up so Google understands exactly what the business does and where. Behind it sits a private documents area, so customers can collect certificates and reports without email attachments.

Key features

What Whitfield Electrical Services does

Local SEO built in

Electrician and LocalBusiness structured data, FAQ markup, a sitemap and Search Console verification help the site appear for local searches.

Designed to get calls

A click-to-call number is never more than a tap away, with “Request a quote” on every screen.

Customer documents area

Customers enter a 6-digit PIN and their surname to download their certificate. Staff upload the file, a unique PIN is generated and it can be emailed to the customer.

Fast on any connection

Plain HTML and CSS with compression and caching rules, so pages load quickly even on mobile data.

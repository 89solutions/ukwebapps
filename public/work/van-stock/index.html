The project

Van stock control, made for the job

Running out of a 15mm elbow halfway through a job means a trip to the merchant, a lost hour and an unhappy customer. Van Stock is a simple stock control app for tradespeople that keeps track of every part on the van, so you know what you’ve got, where it is and what needs ordering.

Getting started takes minutes, not an afternoon of typing. Pick your trade, or several if you work across trades, and Van Stock gives you a ready-made list of the parts you’re likely to carry. Just count what’s there and skip the rest. Then, on the job, scan the barcode on the packet with your phone’s camera, or tap “Use 1”, and your stock updates straight away.

It runs on any smartphone, installs to the home screen like a native app and works with no signal at all, so it’s just as quick in a loft, a basement or a new-build with no mobile coverage. There’s no account to create, and your stock list stays on your phone.

Key features

Everything you need to manage van stock

Barcode scanning

Scan the barcode on a part with your phone camera to record a use, restock it or count it. No extra hardware needed.

Learns your barcodes

The first time a code is scanned, say which part it is, or add it as a new part. Every scan after that finds it instantly.

15 ready-made trade lists

Over 1,800 common parts across 15 trades, from electricians and plumbers to roofers, locksmiths and solar installers.

Mix and match trades

Choose more than one trade and the lists are joined into one, with shared parts shown once. Add from any list later on.

Up to 100 bays

Tell it how many shelves or racking bays your van has, give each part a bay, then filter by bay or search “bay 2”.

One-line quick add

Type “15mm elbow 12” or “twin and earth 50m” and it understands the name, quantity and unit. Or paste a whole list.

“Use 1” with undo

Tap once as you use a part, or enter a larger amount. Every change can be undone if you tap the wrong thing.

Parts recorded per job

Type the current job or address and every part you use is logged against it, which helps with invoicing.

Low-stock warnings

Set a warning level for each part. A badge shows how many are low or out, and one tap lists them all, ready to restock.

Full history

Every use, restock and correction by day, with the job it was for, each one undoable.

Works offline

No signal? No problem. Van Stock works fully offline and installs to your home screen.

Private, with backups

No account and no tracking. Your data stays on your phone, and you can download a backup and restore it on a new phone.

Big buttons for real hands

Large tap targets and clear text, designed to be used quickly with cold or dusty fingers.

Bluetooth scanners too

Prefer a handheld scanner? Pair a Bluetooth barcode scanner, or type the number under the barcode.

How it works

Up and running in minutes

  1. Set up your van

    Say how many bays your van has, or skip bays altogether.

  2. Pick your trades

    Choose one or more ready-made lists and count what you carry. Skip anything you don’t.

  3. Scan or tap on the job

    Scan the part’s barcode or tap “Use 1” as you go, with the job or address noted against each part.

  4. Restock what’s low

    Check the low-stock list before the merchant run, then scan each part as you load up.

Why tradespeople need van stock control

For most trades, the van is the stores. When nobody knows exactly what’s on it, you end up over-ordering “just in case”, carrying dead stock, or making extra trips to the merchant mid-job.

Keeping a simple record of what you carry and what you use cuts wasted journeys, helps you order the right amounts and makes it easy to see which parts went on which job when it’s time to invoice.

Built for the way you work

Van Stock is deliberately simple. There’s no complicated setup and no office software to learn. It does one job well: keeping an accurate count of the parts on your van.

Barcode scanning uses the phone you already carry, with no expensive handheld scanners or labelling. Most parts already have a barcode on the packet; scan it once, tell the app what it is, and from then on a scan is all it takes.

It’s a progressive web app, so it opens in your phone’s browser and can be added to your home screen, where it looks and works like a normal app, with no app store needed.

Van Stock FAQs

How much does Van Stock cost?

Van Stock is a one-off payment of £12. There’s no subscription and no account to set up. Pay securely through Paddle’s checkout and you’ll get your download link straight away.

Can Van Stock scan barcodes?

Yes. Tap the scan button and point your phone’s camera at the barcode on the part or packet. It reads common barcodes including EAN-13, UPC, Code 128, Code 39, ITF, QR codes and Data Matrix. The first time you scan a code, you tell the app which part it is, and it remembers it from then on.

Do I need a barcode scanner?

No. Van Stock uses your phone’s camera, on both iPhone and Android. If you’d rather use a handheld Bluetooth barcode scanner, that works too, and you can always type the number under the barcode.

Does Van Stock work without signal?

Yes. Once it’s been opened, Van Stock works fully offline, including barcode scanning, so you can record parts in lofts, basements and sites with no coverage.

Which trades does it support?

It has ready-made lists for 15 trades, including electrician, plumber, heating engineer, gas engineer, carpenter, decorator, plasterer, tiler, roofer, air con, fire and security, data and network, solar PV and EV chargers, handyman and locksmith. You can combine several lists or start from a blank one.

Can I use it on more than one phone?

Your stock is saved on the phone you use it on. You can download a backup at any time and restore it on another phone, for example when you replace yours.

Do I need to download it from an app store?

No. Open it in your phone’s browser and choose “Add to Home Screen”. It then opens like any other app, on both iPhone and Android.

Can Van Stock be customised for my business?

Yes. We can tailor the part lists, add your own branding or build extra features such as sharing stock across a team of vans. Tell us what you need.

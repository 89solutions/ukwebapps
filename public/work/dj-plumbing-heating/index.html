About D&J Plumbing and Heating

D&J Plumbing and Heating are a family-run firm with over 30 years’ experience in boilers, central heating, bathrooms and showers across Newcastle upon Tyne and Northumberland. We first built them an eye-catching animated site, and have since rebuilt it from the ground up.

The new site puts trust first: Gas Safe registration, experience and real photos of finished bathrooms sit right at the top, with a phone number and quote button always in reach.

How it’s built

Under the bonnet

  • HTML & CSS (no framework)
  • Schema.org structured data
  • Image sitemap
  • Responsive images
Key features

What D&J Plumbing and Heating does

Trust at a glance

Gas Safe registration, experience and “local, family-run” are in the first screen, where people decide whether to call.

Gallery of real jobs

Photos of completed work with descriptive file names and alt text, also listed in the image sitemap so they show up in Google Images.

Plumber structured data

Plumber and FAQPage markup gives Google the opening details, services and answers to common questions.

Easy to get in touch

Click-to-call in the header, a free quote button and email address on every screen.

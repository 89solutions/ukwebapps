About us

A small team that loves mixing technologies

The apple and toffee really shouldn’t go together… but they do, and beautifully. That’s how we think about technology too.

Who we are

Toffee Apple Web, trading online as UK Web Apps, is a small, independent web and app development team based in the North East of England. You’ll deal directly with Peter and Ellie Whitfield. Ellie is also a talented wedding and portrait photographer, so we care about how things look as much as how they work.

We started out building SMS and voice applications: text message bookings, receipts, two-factor logins and monitoring alerts. Since then we’ve built websites for trades and shops across the region, order status displays for busy food outlets, and full web and mobile apps.

When you work with us, you talk directly to the people building your website or app. No account managers, no call centres.

What we believe

  • Simple beats clever. We build the simplest thing that solves your problem well.
  • Fast matters. Quick pages rank better and keep visitors around.
  • Everyone should be able to use it. We design for older users, small screens and slow connections.
  • Privacy by design. We collect only what’s needed and look after it properly.
  • You should own your stuff. Your domain, your content, your code.
